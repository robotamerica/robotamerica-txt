# a quick trip

*19 Nov, 2026*

tomorrow i will head to mexico city for a week. i have some business to get done there, but i will have plenty of time for photowalks, drinks with friends, and writing. we also have a company retreat close to mexico city at the end of next week.

i am packing as light as i can for 12 day trip. a pair of overalls, a pair of jeans, two thick t-shirts, five pairs of socks, five pairs of underwear, one fancy long sleeve shirt, a nice light jacket, and a thicker workman's jacket for the cold evenings. the variety is based on necessity. but i have plenty of space left.

with some of that additional space in my backpack, i will bring three cameras: my konica c35ef, my new(ish) nikon zfc with three lenses (16-50mm kit, 33mm viltrox, and a vintage 50mm minolta) and my canon sl1. i recently forked and adapted m-film (a film simulation interface for canon based on magic lantern). i added half-frame photography, a few more film simulations, and darkroom applications. i decided to install this, less for film-like digital photos, and more for the slowing down experience of shooting as if my camera were analogue, using small digital rolls of 12 photos each.

a bit more mindful with my digital photography with the canon. i like that. can't wait for the trip and the wandering and the cantinas and food. hopefully i get some wonderful photos out of it.
